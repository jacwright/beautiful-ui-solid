Why your agent should own your website

A site that only you can change waits for you. One your agent can change keeps up with the business.

Most small sites start strong and drift. The launch week gets every page right, and then the news page stops at spring and the prices fall behind.

Every change waits for a person

Each fix is small. Together they need someone to notice, open the builder, find the page, and remember how it worked last time.

  • A new book needs a page, a cover, and a link from the home page.
  • A price change touches three pages and the sign-up form.

The site isn't hard to change. It's hard to remember to.

Let the agent keep it current

Give your agent the site, and the change is one sentence: "Add the new book to the site, with its cover, and put it first on the home page."
